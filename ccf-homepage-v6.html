About this study
How CCF tracks the AI pilot landscape
What we track
Any AI system deployed or actively piloted by a U.S. state agency — including chatbots, document processing tools, predictive models, and decision-support systems. We do not include federal systems or purely internal administrative tools.
How we collect data
CCF researchers review executive orders, legislative testimony, procurement notices, and agency press releases. We supplement with direct interviews and a voluntary pilot submission program open to any state agency.

What the data shows
Key findings from the 2026 survey

Finding 01
Benefits & eligibility is the overwhelming entry point for state AI.
Nearly a third of all pilots target benefits delivery — SNAP, Medicaid, unemployment. States are betting AI can reduce backlogs that leave eligible families waiting weeks for assistance.
Health & Human Services
Finding 02
Most pilots bypass public disclosure entirely.
Fewer than 40% of the AI systems we tracked appeared in any public-facing announcement. CCF identified the majority through procurement filings, vendor contracts, and legislative testimony.
Governance & Transparency
Finding 03
Pilot programs stall — but the ones that don't scale fast.
The gap between "piloting" and "live" is the hardest transition. States that cross it tend to expand quickly — but the majority of pilots remain in an indefinite pilot phase for 12+ months.

About CCF
We help government leaders move from exploration to evidence — and evidence into practice.
The Center for Civic Futures is a non-partisan, non-profit initiative of Digital Harbor Foundation. We work directly with state and local government agencies to identify, test, and scale technology-enabled approaches to public service delivery.
The AI Knowledge Hub is our flagship research program — a living effort to document what governments are actually doing with AI, not just what they say they're planning to do. Our researchers review procurement records, attend legislative hearings, and conduct direct interviews with agency staff across the country.
