Benefits Eligibility Screening · Medicaid Redetermination AI
AI synthesis

Both pilots use AI for benefits eligibility decision support with human-in-the-loop review — but they differ in scope and trigger. Colorado focuses on SNAP and Medicaid application pre-screening, processing 2,000 applications per week with a 35% speed improvement. Oregon addresses Medicaid redeterminations following the end of continuous enrollment, managing a surge in caseload without additional staff. Both require caseworker sign-off before any final determination. Oregon has invested more heavily in external equity auditing; Colorado's oversight model is internally led.
