About the AI Knowledge Hub
We help decision-makers move ideas from exploration to evidence — and evidence into practice.
Outdated systems, shrinking budgets, and shifting policies make it hard to plan ahead. The result is a daily balancing act between ambition and capacity, risk and responsibility.
We provide trusted spaces for government leaders to learn, test, and act. Through pilots, partnerships, and shared insight, we track what states are actually deploying — and what's working.
