Benefits Eligibility Screening
Colorado is using AI to help caseworkers process SNAP and Medicaid applications faster — without removing humans from the decision.

The problem
Caseworkers were spending too much time on paperwork, not people
Colorado's Department of Human Services processes hundreds of thousands of SNAP and Medicaid applications each year. Caseworkers were spending significant time on initial document review — verifying submission completeness, cross-referencing eligibility criteria — before they could focus on the judgment calls that require human expertise.
The approach
AI handles the initial read. Humans make every final call.
The pilot uses a fine-tuned language model to review submitted application documents, extract key eligibility data points, and flag cases that need closer review. The system runs alongside existing caseworker workflows — it does not replace them.

Every AI-flagged determination is validated by a human reviewer before a decision is made. The pilot has maintained a zero false negative rate — no eligible applicants have been incorrectly screened out.
Outcomes so far
Early results are strong — and unusually well documented
Processing time for standard applications dropped by 35%. Caseworkers report spending more time on complex cases that require nuanced judgment.

Governance
Every determination is logged, reviewed, and auditable
The pilot operates under the state's AI governance framework with monthly review cycles. An oversight committee — including agency leadership, IT, legal counsel, and community advocates — reviews system performance on an ongoing basis.
